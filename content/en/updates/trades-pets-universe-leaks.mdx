If a claim survives all five steps, it is reasonable to act on it cautiously. If it fails even one, treat it as entertainment rather than information.

## Red Flags: Fake Codes, Scam Links, and Exploit Scripts

Not every bad leak is harmless misinformation. Some exist purely to separate players from their accounts or their pets. The pattern is consistent: urgency, a shortcut, and a request for something valuable in return.

| Red Flag | What It Looks Like | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| "Free pets" generators | Site asks you to log in or verify | Account theft |
| Copy-paste "leak codes" | Random strings with no official source | Never redeemable |
| Executor or script downloads | Promises auto-farm, auto-hatch, auto-delete | Malware plus account ban |
| Guaranteed profit offers | "Trade now, I promise it doubles" | Market manipulation |
| DM pressure | "Do it before it gets patched tonight" | Classic scam tactic |

The script category deserves special attention. Pages that bundle auto-farm, auto-hatch, and auto-equip tools for pet games are not updates — they are third-party software, and downloading them is a direct violation of the [Roblox Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846-Roblox-Terms-of-Use). Beyond the ban risk, these downloads are a common delivery method for credential-stealing malware. No pet in any game is worth your account.
