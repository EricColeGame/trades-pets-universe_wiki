## FAQ

**How often does the trades pets universe latest update change pet values?**
Values shift most sharply in the first few days after any update, then stabilize over one to two weeks. After that, prices usually move gradually until the next event or content drop.

**Should I trade my new pets immediately or hold them?**
If you hatched a brand-new pet on day one, trading early often gets you a premium from impatient collectors. If you are unsure, waiting 48 to 72 hours gives you a clearer picture of real value.

**What is the safest way to trade pets?**
Always use the in-game trade window, screenshot significant deals, and decline anything involving trust trades or account sharing. No legitimate trade requires you to hand over account access.

**How do I know if a trade is fair?**
Compare rarity tier, current demand, supply trend, and recent completed trades — not just one value list. If the offer feels unclear or rushed, walk away and revisit it later.

**Do old pets still matter after an update?**
Yes. Discontinued and event-limited pets often gain value over time because their supply is fixed. They are usually better long-term holds than newly released pets with collapsing debut prices.
