### Avoiding Scams and Bad Deals

Trade scams rely on confusion: a fast countdown, a last-second swap, or a "trust trade" that asks you to hand something over first. There is no legitimate reason for anyone to ask you to trade a pet away with nothing in return.

Keep your account secure, never share login details, and treat any offer that requires you to leave the trade window as a red flag.
